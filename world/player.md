# Player

name: Xu Ping (徐平) — roughly *shü ping*
situation: Early sixties. Keeps a physician's stall in the West Market, Chang'an.
  His own man, barely. Mostly a loner.
wants: something is pressing and he has not let himself name it.
  GENUINELY UNDETERMINED — not decided in advance by anyone, including the narrator.
  What it turns out to be gets built out of what he does about it.
bad at: patients. Bored by common ailments, impatient with the people who have them.
  His consultations have shrunk from an hour to a quarter of one; he would rather
  give five minutes. He calls this efficiency.

  Eleven days ago he cut a blank slip and set it aside to write something on. It is
  still blank. He has not looked at why.

## Carrying
- a physician's roll: needles in a bamboo case, a small knife for cutting root
- a bronze steelyard and weights, jin and liang
- mortar, pestle, three sieves of different mesh
- mugwort punk for moxa, wrapped in oiled cloth
- a bundle of wooden slips, corded, in his own hand — forty years of case notes
- a brush, ink cake, water dropper

## Money
a little over two thousand coins, in strings and a loose handful, under the
floorboard of the stall. Not poverty. Not one bad season from safety either.

- materia medica: expert — can name a decoction's ingredients by smell alone
- pulse diagnosis: skilled — forty years of wrists
- reading and copying texts: skilled — he has copied more than he has been taught
- needling: capable — competent, uninterested; he is a drug man
- market trade and haggling: capable — necessity, not talent
- sitting with the frightened: capable — a night at a brazier, asked by a watchman
  how long he had, and he stayed and answered the true question instead of the
  asked one

## Known by
- West Market: a good physician who does not like you. People come anyway.
