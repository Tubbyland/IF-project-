# World

setting: Chang'an, capital of Han. The West Market and the walled wards around it.
era: roughly forty years after the death of the old emperor — the one who pushed the
  borders out and spent the treasury doing it. The wars are over. So is the money.
  Court runs on consort families and eunuch secretaries now. Nothing has fallen;
  everyone can feel the best of it is behind them and is careful not to say so.
scale: one city. Mostly two market wards and the streets between them.

tone: Scandinavian detective. Procedural pleasure and quiet dread running side by
  side on the same page — competence is what the reader enjoys, deterioration is
  what they dread. Rich in the detail of the craft: ingredients, weights,
  processing, diagnosis, rendered with the delight Xu Ping takes in them.

what can exist: AMBIGUOUS. Permanently, in both directions, and there is no hidden
  answer held in reserve.
  - Every anomaly carries a mundane explanation available at the same moment as
    the other reading. Both stay open.
  - Anomalies are small and deniable: a count that will not reconcile, a recovery
    on the wrong timetable, a symptom arriving out of order. Never an apparition.
  - He barely notices. He notices late, or not at all.
  - RECORDING RULE: write down what was observed. Never what it meant.

what magic/technology costs: nothing is established as operative, so nothing has a
  price yet. Do not invent one.

The medicine of the period is intact and not in question: qi, the five phases, the
correspondences of organ and season and flavour, wind as a vector of illness. That
is the frame Xu Ping thinks in and the language his expertise is expressed in.

off the table: nothing declared. The player may add to this at any time without
  it being a moment.

play style: two or three options and a "Something else?" at the close of a scene.
  When narrative energy flags, move in time or space rather than finishing a scene
  out of duty. Substantial scenes, not terse ones. Player steers as we go.
  Stay inside the fiction. Xu Ping has interior thoughts — use them. Do not step
  out to comment as narrator unless the player asks a direct question.
