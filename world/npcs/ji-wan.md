# Ji Wan
Drug merchant, the sellers' row, West Market. Fifties. The largest stock in the ward
— three times Xu Ping's frontage, drawers to the roof, two boys running for him.

stage: resistant

voice: never looks at his hands while they work. Weighs, wraps, cords and knots the
packet with his eyes on your face the whole time. Courteous in a way that is worse
than being insulted.

knows: that Xu Ping broke three years of silence to walk into his stall and buy musk.
  Does NOT know why. Does NOT know about Guo Lan, the robe, or the fourth smell.

owes/owed: nothing. Xu Ping paid.

toward player: has not forgiven him. Three years ago Xu Ping said out loud, in the
  row, in front of customers, that Ji Wan's aconite was under-processed. It was.
  Being right is the part that has not been forgiven.

last seen: his stall, the day the aconite was cut.

observed, not explained: holds musk in quantity during a shortage the perfumers have
  been complaining of all winter; prices it under what that shortage should command;
  the grade is better than any Xu Ping has handled in forty years.
