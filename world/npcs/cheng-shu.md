# Cheng Shu
Second-hand clothes dealer, third stall in the north lane, West Market. Past seventy.

stage: cracked

voice: prices things aloud whether or not they are for sale. Not a tactic — a
condition. Valued Xu Ping's own robe twice in one conversation.

knows: that Xu Ping is chasing a silk robe that smells of a prescription, and that
  musk is involved. Told him Ji Wan has musk. Does NOT know about the Geng house.

owes/owed: nothing.

toward player: amused by him. Enjoys being consulted. No loyalty either way.

last seen: his stall, the morning he cut the aconite.
