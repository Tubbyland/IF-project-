# The Geng house
Fourth ward east of the West Market, three doors along from the ward gate. A family
with a name and a salary. Retiled expensively about fifteen years ago, untouched
since. Weeds at the base of the east wall where a gardener used to be.

controlled by: the Geng family. Master Geng Su, an official, died some weeks ago.

changed: nothing yet — Xu Ping has only stood outside it.

hidden here:
observed, not explained:
- undyed hemp at the gate posts, weathered grey along the folds. Weeks old.
- the burial is delayed. Stated reason: a son travelling back from a provincial post.
- four liang of musk delivered every ninth day, ongoing.
- a physician still attends, by carriage.
- the dead man's silk robe went out to a seamstress to be cut down before he is
  in the ground.
