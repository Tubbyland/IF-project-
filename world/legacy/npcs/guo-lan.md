# Guo Lan
Seamstress, West Market ward. Mid-thirties, thin through the shoulders. Flattened
fingerpads, callus at the base of the middle finger.

stage: resistant

voice: answers the exact question and nothing adjacent. Apologises when she is not
sorry. Goes quiet rather than refuse outright.

knows: that Xu Ping took an unusual interest in her sleeve and did not say why.
  Does NOT know he can name a decoction by smell. Does NOT know anything is
  considered wrong. Believes she has an ordinary cough, which she has.

owes/owed: paid in full for the cough remedy — apricot kernel, liquorice.

toward player: wary-polite. Will not name who gave her the private sewing work.
  Stated fear: her shop master learning she takes side work, and the work stopping.

last seen: his stall, the morning he cut the aconite.

observed, not explained: her sleeves carry liquorice, cinnamon bark of good grade,
  and musk. And a fourth thing he could not name.
