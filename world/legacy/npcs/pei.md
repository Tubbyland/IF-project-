# Pei
Steward of the Geng house. Forties, grey at the temple. Twenty years with the family.
The face of a man who has slept in pieces for six weeks. Tang: "the look of a man
carrying a table on his own."

stage: resistant

voice: says "the house" where another man would say we, or the family. Never opens a
door more than the width of his own shoulder.

knows: Xu Ping's name, trade, and that he can smell something from the gutter he
  cannot name — Xu Ping told him all of it, at the gate, truthfully. Does NOT know
  he followed the musk delivery, spoke to Tang, or knelt at the east wall.

owes/owed: nothing.

toward player: refused him at the gate and barred it. Not hostile. Rehearsed.

last seen: his own doorway, at the second drum.

observed, not explained: before refusing, before asking anything else, he said
  "Did he send you?"
